## English prompt（給製作流程用，可存檔重複使用）

Create a 60-second recruitment video, coded and rendered programmatically, for junior-high students considering the
Department of Information Technology at Example Senior High School (範例高中資訊科).
Working title: **"{標題：見下方三選一}"**.

Tone: energetic, hands-on and proud — make a 14-year-old think *"that looks fun, I want to try it"* and *"wow, they're that good."*
Narrated by a friendly senior student ("學長姐") speaking directly to the viewer in Traditional Chinese, with bold on-screen captions;
every scene cut lands on the beat of a driving, high-energy track.

1. **Hook (0–5s)**: A terminal types `print("Hello, 未來的學弟妹")` — on Enter, the screen explodes into the title.
2. **Make Things Move (≈20s)**: Coding in C and Python; an AI model recognising objects on camera; ESP32/Arduino projects lighting up sensors;
   a collaborative robot arm picking and placing a block on command.
3. **Built to Win (≈16s)**: 10 WorldSkills international competitors, 12 national gold medals, world-stage finishes (Lyon 2024, world medals);
   56th Skills Competition — regional gold/silver/bronze sweep in two trades, plus a national bronze.
4. **Where It Leads (≈8s)**: 26 admissions to national universities — Taiwan Tech, YunTech, NKUST flash on screen.
5. **Call (≈6s)**: School name + slogan, final beat hit.

Visuals: every fact becomes a moving visual — typing code windows, animated counters, path animations, objects that react on the beat.
No static slides. Big, readable Chinese text for a large hall screen and for muted YouTube viewing.
Audio: high-energy track with crisp impacts, risers into each section, counter blips, and a clean narration bed (music ducks under the voice).
Avoid: exam/bonus-point jargon (技優甄審、統測), teacher names, room numbers, any claim not in the fact sheet.

## 中文對照（重點）
- **開場**：終端機打字 `print("Hello, 未來的學弟妹")`，按下 Enter 後畫面炸開，接標題
- **段 1　動手做（約 20 秒）**：C／Python 寫程式、AI 辨識畫面、ESP32 讓感測器亮起來、機械手臂聽指令夾方塊
- **段 2　有多強（約 16 秒）**：10 位國際國手、12 面全國金牌、世界賽成績、56 屆分區賽雙職類包辦＋全國賽銅牌
- **段 3　升學（約 8 秒）**：26 人次國立大專，臺科、雲科、高科校名快閃
- **結尾（約 6 秒）**：校名＋口號，最後一個重拍收住
- **不放**：加分制度術語、老師姓名、教室號碼，以及事實清單以外的任何說法
